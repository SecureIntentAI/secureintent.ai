MCP and agent security

In development

AI is moving past copy and paste. Assistants and agents now connect directly to files, databases, services and internal tools through MCP (Model Context Protocol), a new boundary where sensitive data can leak without anyone pasting anything. SecureIntent is extending the same detect and sanitise guardrail to that layer: discovering MCP connections, telling approved servers from unapproved ones, and applying policy so agents stay productive without exposing what they should not.
