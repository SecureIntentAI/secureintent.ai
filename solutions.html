Protection that keeps up with how you use AI.

Browser Extension

The fastest way to make AI safe to use.

Available now

SecureIntent watches for secrets, credentials, API keys, tokens, JWTs and sensitive data the moment you paste, and steps in before anything reaches the model. You keep working at full speed while it does the checking for you.

Local detection

RegEx and entropy scoring catch API keys, tokens, JWTs, credentials and PII on device, so you never stop to self-censor.

Intercept before paste

Sensitive content is caught before it reaches ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot and other tools, without breaking your flow.

Anonymise and Paste

Swap sensitive values for safe placeholders in one click and keep moving.

Dehydrate and Rehydrate

Salts and anonymises tokens on the way out, then restores them locally when the model replies, so answers stay usable.

Ghost Log Sanitiser

Strips secrets out of logs and stack traces before you share them for debugging.

Session Lock

Protects cloud-console and sensitive sessions from accidental exposure.

Built for flow

Runs silently in the background and never arbitrarily blocks your prompt.

Zero raw-data retention

Evaluation happens on device; your raw pasted text never leaves your machine.

Developer Desktop Application

Protection beyond the browser, everywhere you work.

Beta · September

AI has moved out of the browser. SecureIntent Desktop brings the same protection to terminals, IDEs, desktop AI apps and the system clipboard, so your whole workflow is covered without slowing you down.

System-wide clipboard protection

Redacts secrets on copy across every app, so you are protected in terminals, IDEs and desktop AI tools, not just the browser.

Terminal-aware

Understands terminal copy-on-select so command-line secrets are handled correctly.

JWT detect and decode

Spots and decodes tokens so you can see what is sensitive at a glance.

Undo and restore

Every redaction is recoverable, so protection never costs you work.

OS-level gating

Sanitises PII, IP and secrets before they leave the machine, regardless of the target app.

Redaction log

A local record of what was protected, so you and your team can review activity.

MCP and agent security

In development

AI is moving past copy and paste. Assistants and agents now connect directly to your files, databases, services and internal tools through MCP (Model Context Protocol), which creates a new boundary where sensitive data can leak without anyone pasting anything. SecureIntent is extending its security model to that layer: discovering MCP connections, telling approved servers from unapproved ones, and applying policy so agents stay productive without exposing what they should not.
