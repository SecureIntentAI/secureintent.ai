Cybersecurity Pedigree

SecureIntent exists so organisations can let their teams use AI without leaking secrets, credentials and sensitive data. Protection and visibility, without getting in the way. The people behind it:

Board

SM

The Rt Hon Stephen McPartland

Non-Executive Chairman

Stephen McPartland chairs the SecureIntent board. A former UK Government minister with responsibility for security, he authored the McPartland Review into cyber security, a government-commissioned review of how the UK strengthens its cyber resilience. He brings national-security perspective and public-policy experience to SecureIntent's mission of making AI safe to adopt.
